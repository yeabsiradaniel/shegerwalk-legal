ShegerWalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Information We Collect

ShegerWalk collects health and fitness data (such as step count) to track your walking progress and calculate rewards. We also collect your phone number, name, and profile information when you create an account.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the move-to-earn service, process rewards, send notifications, and improve the app.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the app.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data (such as step counts, rewards, and profile information) for as long as your account is active to provide you with our services. We may also retain data as necessary to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the ShegerWalk app by navigating to Profile > Delete Account. Alternatively, you can submit a data deletion request by contacting us via the email below.
